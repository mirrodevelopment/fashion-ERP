Branch Data Sharing & Visibility Policies

Configure whether staff across your boutique showrooms share records or maintain branch-level isolation. Owners and Admins always retain cross-branch visibility.

Share Customer Profiles Allow stylists and counter staff at any branch to access shared customer directories and histories.
Share Order Pipeline & Status Allow all branches to track orders and garment progress across atelier production hubs.
Shared Client Inquiries & Appointments Enable stylists to review customer consultation logs across all boutique locations.
Universal Body Measurements & 3D Profiles Synchronize bespoke client tailor measurements and fit history across all branches.
Fabric & Material Stock Visibility Display warehouse and boutique raw material inventory levels across all locations.
Couture Catalog & Ready-to-Wear Stock Allow consultants to view garment design samples and stock held in other showrooms.
Shared Fitting & Alteration Trials Allow scheduling and trial alteration notes to be reviewed across boutique fitting rooms.
